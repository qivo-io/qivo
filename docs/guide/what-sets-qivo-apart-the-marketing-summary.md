Qivo combines task tracking with weekly planning for people and AI agents.
Its main features are:

- **Delay forecasts.** Remaining work and the owner's available capacity
  determine whether a task is likely to miss its plan or due week ([Delay projections](./will-it-be-ready-in-time-the-delay-status.md#how-the-projection-works)).
- **Live workload.** Scheduling a task updates its owner's weekly load ([The Team strip](./planning-against-real-capacity.md#the-team-strip)).
- **Parent schedules.** Scheduled parents expand to cover their scheduled
  subtasks, including date changes through the API ([Scheduling by hand](./the-roadmap.md#scheduling-by-hand)).
- **Permanent task IDs.** Moving a task keeps its number and links ([Permanent task IDs](./how-work-is-organized.md#permanent-task-ids)).
- **Private workload.** For organization admins and standard users, hidden
  projects contribute anonymous load without revealing their tasks ([The Team strip](./planning-against-real-capacity.md#the-team-strip)).
- **Assistants and agents.** MCP acts with a person's or agent's permissions
  within one organization. REST uses named agent accounts. Task changes record
  the actor and connection type ([Automation and AI](./automation-ai-the-rest-api-and-the-mcp-server.md)).
- **Weekly calendars.** The organization sets its first workday and
  week-numbering rule ([Calendar weeks](./the-roadmap.md#weeks-that-match-your-calendar)).
- **Grouped task cards.** The Board keeps subtasks with their parent within
  each column and lane. Stale tasks fade and paused tasks show a mark ([The Board](./the-board.md)).
- **Task history.** Comments and changes share an activity feed, including
  description-edit excerpts and attribution for comment edits ([Your inbox](./working-together.md#your-inbox)).
- **Project guests.** Share specific projects with external collaborators.
  Shared projects appear beside their other work. An active home membership
  exempts a guest from your user charge ([Guest billing](./who-sees-what-the-access-model.md#people-outside-your-organization)).
